# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in `craftctl`
(`https://github.com/ferranox/craftctl`) a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

- Be kind, patient, and constructive.
- Welcome newcomers; assume good faith.
- Focus on the technical problem; no personal attacks, trolling, or hate speech.
- Respect differing viewpoints and admit when you are wrong.

## Enforcement

Unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the maintainers via a GitHub issue
or the contact listed in `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers may warn, temporarily ban,
or permanently remove contributors who violate this code.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions)
and when representing the project in public.
